# PRIME Detailing — Brand Guide

## Positioning
Premium mobile interior detailing for customers in Zwolle and nearby areas. The brand should feel precise, dark, automotive and cared-for.

## Visual language
- Background: near-black / charcoal
- Accent: warm metallic gold
- Text: off-white; muted warm gray for secondary copy
- Cards: dark surfaces with fine borders, not heavy shadows
- Corners: modern, controlled rounding
- Motion: subtle, slow, deliberate

## Keywords
Premium, clean, precise, dark, automotive, calm, detailed, trustworthy.

## Avoid
Cheap wash-bay visuals, bright blue SaaS gradients, oversaturated colors, cartoon icons, excessive glowing effects, fake luxury clichés, crowded sections.
